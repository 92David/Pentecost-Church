About the Church

Church History

1998

Church Founded

Pentecost Church began as a small gathering of believers committed to worship, prayer, and the Word of God.

2000

First Church Building

The church took its first step into a permanent place of worship, creating room for growth and fellowship.

2005

First Major Expansion

The church experienced significant growth through outreach, discipleship, and a renewed focus on serving the community.

2012

Youth Ministry Started

A dedicated youth ministry was established to help young people grow in faith, leadership, and purpose.

2018

New Branch Established

The church expanded its reach by establishing a new branch to serve more families and communities.

2026

Church Today

Today, Pentecost Church continues to grow in faith, worship, service, and impact across generations.
