Giving

Support the mission and ministry of Pentecost Church

Tithe

First Fruits
  • Honors God’s faithfulness and generosity.
  • Helps sustain church operations and discipleship.
  • Supports pastoral care, worship, and ministry outreach.

General Offering

Church Support
  • Helps meet the everyday needs of the church.
  • Supports prayer meetings, preaching, and fellowship.
  • Strengthens the overall mission of the church family.

Thanksgiving Offering

Gratitude
  • Expresses gratitude for God’s goodness and mercy.
  • Celebrates answered prayers and divine provision.
  • Encourages a heart of worship and thanksgiving.

Missions Offering

Reach the World
  • Supports evangelism and global mission outreach.
  • Helps churches, leaders, and communities in need.
  • Advances the gospel across cities and nations.

Building/Development

Church Growth
  • Contributes to church expansion and development projects.
  • Supports safer, better, and more welcoming spaces.
  • Helps prepare facilities for future generations.

Benevolence/Welfare

Care & Support
  • Provides help for families and individuals in need.
  • Supports emergency and welfare assistance initiatives.
  • Shows Christ’s love through practical care.

Special Offering

Seasonal Support
  • Supports special church initiatives and campaigns.
  • Helps fund events, outreach, and community programs.
  • Strengthens combined effort during special moments.

Other Giving

Personal Contribution
  • For additional contributions outside the listed categories.
  • Lets you support the church in a way that fits your need.
  • Useful for personalized acts of generosity and support.
