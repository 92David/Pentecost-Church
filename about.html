Vision & Mission

To see lives transformed by the Gospel, families strengthened in faith, and the community reached with the hope of Christ.

Statement of Faith

We believe in the Trinity, the authority of Scripture, the power of prayer, the saving grace of Jesus, and the Holy Spirit’s active work in the lives of believers.

Core Values

Worship, prayer, discipleship, outreach, generosity, integrity, and Christ-centered love guide every part of our church life.

Pastor's Message

Our pastor leads with a heart for revival, practical teaching, and a deep commitment to serving people with grace and truth.

Church Leadership

The leadership team serves with humility, wisdom, and accountability to help the church remain strong, united, and spiritually focused.

Our Location

Join us at 123 Grace Avenue, where worship, fellowship, and community outreach continue to thrive.
