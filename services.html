Services

Join us for worship, study, and prayer.

Sunday Worship

A joyful and Spirit-filled Sunday gathering with prayer, praise, testimony, preaching, and an opportunity to respond to God's Word.

A Bible-centered teaching hour for all ages, helping believers grow in understanding, discipleship, and practical Christian living.

Interactive teaching and discussion that helps members deepen their understanding of Scripture and apply it to everyday life.

A dedicated time of intercession, spiritual renewal, and seeking God's guidance for the church, the community, and individuals.

A powerful prayer and worship gathering held at night for revival, healing, and a fresh move of the Holy Spirit.

Special Services

Seasonal and special church gatherings designed for revival, celebration, outreach, and spiritual renewal.
